# Clinic reply policy

## Objective

- Reduce physician workload.
- Standardize patient communication.
- Reduce patient waiting time.
- Prepare a ready-to-review reply draft within one hour.
- Invite the patient to the clinic instead of providing a remote medical
  consultation.

## Required controls

- Work with one designated Yandex 360 business mailbox.
- Save replies in Yandex Mail Drafts.
- Require a human manager to review, edit when needed, and send every reply.
- Do not enable automatic sending.
- Do not include unsupported medical, pricing, scheduling, or organizational
  claims.

## Writing style

- Use polite, calm, plain Russian.
- Acknowledge the patient's question without overstating certainty.
- Keep the response short enough for email.
- Give a clear next step: contact the clinic or arrange an in-person visit.
- Do not invent a greeting name, clinic signature, phone number, address,
  doctor, appointment slot, price, or preparation instruction.

## Escalate instead of answering

Prepare only a neutral acknowledgement and invitation for human review when a
message contains urgent symptoms, medication questions, treatment changes,
post-operative complications, test interpretation, legal complaints, payment
disputes, or unclear consent and identity details.
